## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are hiring managers, engineering leaders, recruiters, and founders evaluating Shaun Alonzo for full-time senior software engineering roles, with contract, consulting, and fractional engineering as secondary options.

## Product Purpose

`shaunalonzo.com` is Shaun Alonzo's static personal website. It presents his engineering expertise, team impact, work history, featured projects, location, and contact paths so qualified hiring teams can evaluate him for a full-time senior engineering role. Contract and consulting conversations remain supported as secondary paths.

Success means visitors quickly understand Shaun's fit for a full-time senior .NET/AWS role, trust the evidence shown on the site, and contact him by email or through LinkedIn/GitHub.

## Positioning

Shaun is positioned as a Senior .NET & AWS Engineer pursuing full-time team opportunities. He modernizes legacy .NET systems, designs cloud-native AWS architectures, and helps engineering teams adopt AI-augmented engineering workflows. Contract and consulting work are secondary options.

## Operating Context

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages at `shaunalonzo.com` and is hand-maintained as a static web presence. Visitors may arrive directly, through search/social previews, or from shared links to routed sections such as About, Services, Skills, Experience, Projects, and Contact.

Shaun is based in Manila, Philippines (UTC+8) and works remotely with teams in Australia, the UK, and the US.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static single-page website with client-side section routing and a GitHub Pages `404.html` fallback.
- No package manager, build step, or test suite; preview locally with `python3 -m http.server 8000`.
- Keep paths relative for GitHub Pages compatibility.
- Keep the custom domain configuration in `CNAME` intact unless explicitly changed.
- Contact email is `contact@shaunalonzo.com`.
- The site includes visible contact actions, LinkedIn/GitHub links, dark/light theme support, responsive navigation, analytics events, and a static chat assistant.
- Personal, career, contact, metadata, structured data, and chat-response facts must stay consistent when updated.
