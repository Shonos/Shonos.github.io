# Shaun Alonzo

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: founders, small businesses, and teams adopting AI who need a hands-on engineer to understand and solve an operational or product problem. Secondary: managers hiring a senior software engineer.

## Product Purpose

Help a visitor understand what Shaun can solve, evaluate credible evidence, and email him about a project or full-time role. Clients take priority without hiding the hiring path.

## Positioning

Practical AI and software engineering grounded in problem diagnosis, tradeoffs, implementation, verification, and handover. Core stack: AI, .NET/C#, SQL, AWS, React/TypeScript. One professional identity, not separate consultant and employee personas.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static Vite site on GitHub Pages. Content is maintained in JSON and rendered at build time.
- Three offers: workflow automation; product/system improvements including useful AI features; engineering-team AI adoption.
- Email is the primary inquiry channel. No form backend, scheduler, public AI chatbot, or new demo backend.
- Preserve light/dark preferences, accessible interactions, legacy section links, the custom domain, and local analytics suppression.
- Implementation is for local review. Do not commit, push, or deploy without explicit authorization.

## Evidence on Hand

Career records in the separate `shauna-career` repository distinguish recollection from CV-derived claims. Use public-safe summaries only; never bundle private evidence or internal source documents. Application modernization, reusable HTTP integrations, React delivery, engineering AI tooling, and a personal credit-card information project are supported. No unmeasured AI productivity claims, absolute zero-impact claims, unsupported Principal title, or invented SQL-tuning results. Availability, confidential details, employment-date conflicts, and publication of new metrics remain unconfirmed. Do not invent a downloadable résumé.

## Product Principles

- Business problem before technology inventory.
- Demonstrate decisions and personal ownership, not just finished artifacts.
- Distinguish professional work, personal projects, and illustrative workflows.
- Prefer the simplest effective solution; AI is a tool, not a mandatory ingredient.
- Keep project and full-time inquiries easy to distinguish.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Responsive and keyboard-operable, readable without JavaScript, reduced-motion aware, meaningful image alternatives, WCAG AA color contrast, and usable at 320px width.
