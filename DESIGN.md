# Design System: Shaun Alonzo — Independent Engineering Practice

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Technical Decision Brief"**

A calm, client-first engineering practice expressed as a visual decision brief: cool white, deep green ink, controlled teal, and confident Manrope headings. Large media frames, aligned conceptual geometry, open rows, and crisp rules make the work understandable before asking for extended reading. Expression comes from composition, with finite movement explaining the approved migration handover or deliberate AI workflow selection and replay, not ornamental effects.

The same visual identity supports practical AI, software delivery, and the full-time hiring path. Professional work, personal experiments, and illustrative workflows remain visibly distinct. Semantic conceptual frames stand in honestly until approved screenshots or recordings are supplied; they are not fabricated product evidence or live activity. This is a code-led extension of the existing world, with no generated comp or imagery; the user-supplied portrait is reused without visual changes.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Light-led green neutrals with a complete dark-theme counterpart.
- One self-hosted variable family, large left-aligned headings, and compact contextual metadata.
- Alternating media/copy rows, compact summaries, thin dividers, and flat tinted frames.
- Filled email actions, quieter text links, and native document navigation.
- Semantic conceptual geometry, explicitly scoped evidence, and a manually explorable workflow.
- Visible-by-default content, fixed migration boxes, one-pass illustrative traffic handover, explicit AI workflow replay, and reduced-motion parity.

## Colors

Cool near-whites and green-black text lead; related green fields establish evidence and inquiry contexts rather than a second accent family. Frontmatter names without a prefix match the light-theme CSS custom properties. `dark-` entries are the replacements assigned by `data-theme="dark"`, not extra simultaneous brand colors.

### Primary
- **Controlled Teal** (`accent` / `dark-accent`): filled primary actions, selected headline emphasis, links, active navigation, small diagram/check icons, focus outlines, and selection backgrounds.
- **Action Hover** (`accent-hover` / `dark-accent-hover`): darker in light mode, lighter in dark mode. Button text uses the corresponding `button-ink`, not a fixed white in both themes.
- **Evidence Pair** (`proof` + `proof-ink`, with dark replacements): conceptual media fields, integration emphasis, and contact. Contact, serving migration systems, selected migration stages, and the selected AI workflow node reverse this pair.

The AI field retains local pale-green overrides for keyboard focus (`#a7e1c4`), evidence classification (`#b5e8ce`), and links (`#c8eed9`). These are scoped component details, not additional general-purpose accents. Selected workflow detail and its counter use `deep-ink` on `deep-line` (6.07:1); do not substitute `deep-muted` on that fill. Real image/video backgrounds remain white to preserve source artwork rather than recoloring it with the theme. Sidecar previews bind to CSS variables; synthesized eight-step OKLCH strips are panel metadata only, not a shipped tonal scale.

Light is the CSS baseline. The early theme script respects saved light/dark choice, otherwise the operating-system preference; subsequent explicit choices persist. Do not force light mode over a visitor's preference.

**The Evidence Pair Rule.** Tinted evidence and inquiry fields always use their paired ink; dark AI evidence uses its own foreground and divider colors.

## Typography

**Display, body, and labels:** Manrope variable Latin, self-hosted at `public/assets/fonts/manrope-latin.woff2`, preloaded in the document head, declared for weights (400–800) with `font-display: swap`. The bundled `OFL-Manrope.txt` records the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Fallbacks are the system sans-serif stack in the frontmatter; there is no separate mono face.

**Character:** tightly tracked, balanced headings over airy reading text. The build uses intermediate variable weights (600, 650, 700, 750) as well as regular (400), not a separate font for technical labels. This is an observed role-based scale, not a mathematical modular ratio. Rem-to-pixel examples below assume the browser's default root size (16px).

### Hierarchy
- **Display:** the `display` token governs the homepage statement, capped at (12ch). At the supplied desktop capture width (1440px), the reported live value is (76.32px), not the tiny apparent size of a full-page thumbnail. The closing phrase is a muted block within the heading using `display-close`, with top separation (18px) and a (24ch) measure.
- **Headline:** `headline` governs main section headings, approximately (46px) at the same desktop width. Most split headings are limited to (18ch); local copy-led limits vary. Headings have balanced wrapping, not uppercase kickers or decorative underlines.
- **Title:** `title` is the usual offer/AI story heading. `work-title` gives media-led work previews their larger responsive title; `concept-title` gives the frame its own compact headline. Workflow introduction uses (1.5rem), process titles (1.125rem), and the toolkit heading (1.0625rem). These are sentence-case headings, not added kickers.
- **Reading display:** `case-display` introduces a case with a (22ch) measure. `profile-display` introduces the engineering profile. Case section labels use `case-section`; profile section headings use (1.75rem). These are intentionally smaller than homepage persuasion headings.
- **Body:** `body` is the (16px) baseline, with paragraphs limited to (70ch). Hero description has line-height (1.8) and a (58ch) limit. Case/profile summaries use `intro`; desktop section introductions use (1.0625rem). Service, work, AI, and case prose commonly use `body-compact`; approach and profile role descriptions use `body-small`.
- **Actions and navigation:** `label` covers primary button text; default text links share its size/weight but inherit body line-height. Navigation uses its own (13px) role, with a minimum link target height (44px). Wordmark weight is (750); tag/stack and result emphasis can be (650).
- **Contextual metadata:** `metadata` covers inline technologies, portrait captions, and case category labels. `metadata-small` covers evidence classification, work categories, and footer copy. The existing `caption` role is employer context only; it is not a diagram disclaimer size. `media-caption` uses (12px / 1.6), and review-slot notes also stay at (12px). Conceptual node labels/details use `concept-label` / `concept-detail`; at or below (800px), both remain at least (12px). Small supporting metadata is not a substitute for reading copy.

At or below (800px), the hero heading becomes (3.1rem), work titles (1.5rem), and concept titles (1.25rem). At or below (600px), the hero uses `clamp(2.5rem, 10.7vw, 3.7rem)`, a (13ch) measure, and a closing phrase (0.49em); work titles become (1.3125rem) and concept titles (1.375rem). Main H2s become (2rem); case/profile H1s become (2.375rem). Most compact reading copy becomes (0.875rem), while case/profile summaries become (1rem). The precise component overrides remain in the stylesheet.

**The One Family Rule.** Use Manrope for headings, prose, navigation, and technical context; hierarchy comes from scale, weight, measure, and spacing rather than another display face.

## Elevation & Depth

There are no box shadows, glows, glass layers, or hover-lifting cards in the implemented stylesheet. Depth comes from the page/surface distinction, quiet green washes, the deep AI field, and one-pixel borders. The sticky header sits above content (z-index 30); the focus-only skip link sits above it (100). Neither introduces a separate shadow vocabulary.

Diagram boxes are upright and stable: migration keeps fixed customers/router/old/new nodes, integration retains its centered width hierarchy, and product nodes keep their desktop/device silhouettes without rotations. The approved migration sequence moves SVG traffic and build progress, not panels. There is no automatic hero entrance, work-frame clip-path or transition, or hover- or pointer-move-triggered diagram movement, scaling, or transforms. Content is visible by default; there are no repeated scroll reveals or infinite animations. Separate, explicit AI workflow replay animates its node entrances (650ms), staggered by (65ms). Selecting an AI step adds brief opacity feedback (250ms) without moving the selected node; conceptual nodes transition only background and foreground colors (200ms). Buttons, AI workflow selection fill, and link-arrow response use (180ms); the arrow moves (3px).

CSS transitions and tracked Web Animations use the source easing recorded in the sidecar; the migration's bounded requestAnimationFrame timeline advances linearly. Changing to reduced motion or hiding the document cancels tracked Web Animations and pauses migration playback. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, scrolling becomes immediate, CSS animations/transitions are disabled globally, AI replay and migration transport controls are hidden, and static stage selection remains usable. Migration also shows a reduced-motion explanation. Backgrounding pauses migration and native video without autoresume; it does not erase content or restart illustrations.

**The Flat Brief Rule.** Separate content with whitespace, tonal fields, and thin rules, not shadows, glows, or lifted card shells.

**The Fixed Systems Rule.** Migration motion changes SVG traffic and build progress, never box positions; other conceptual diagrams stay still on hover, with no automatic hero entrance or endless loop.

## Shapes

Media frames, case takeaways, and the desktop portrait use the shared container radius. Filled and outlined buttons use the tighter button radius; the monogram retains `node`. Conceptual nodes and migration system boxes use `concept-node`, the device-shaped product node uses `device`, and selected AI workflow controls and migration stage buttons use `workflow-control`. The migration router uses the button radius. Theme/menu controls, process counters, and node markers are circular; inline technology lists are not pill chips.

Conceptual geometry is explanatory: fixed customers/router nodes connected to equal-width, side-by-side migration systems, a central shared-integration node, paired upright desktop/device silhouettes, or a two-by-two workflow. It is not fake application chrome. Existing local corners remain (3px) for focus, (6px) for the skip link, and (10px) for the mobile portrait. Borders are generally (1px), with stronger outlines reserved for keyboard focus. Icons are inline SVG, not text glyphs or an icon font.

## Components

### Buttons and text links
Confident action, quiet alternatives.
- **Primary:** filled `accent` with paired button ink, minimum height (52px), a transparent one-pixel border, and the primary padding/typography in frontmatter. Hover changes the fill, without a lift. At or below (600px), minimum height becomes (50px), padding (12px 18px), and text (0.8125rem).
- **Contact variant:** reverses the evidence pair; hover applies `brightness(0.9)`. It is an email anchor, not a form submission.
- **Secondary:** transparent, ink text, hairline border, tint hover. Used for printing the engineering profile; narrow profile minimum height is (44px).
- **Text link:** bold teal text with an inline arrow, no resting underline; hover adds underline and moves only the arrow. Smaller contextual variants use (0.75rem–0.8125rem).
- **Focus / disabled:** shared keyboard outline (3px) with offset (5px); on the deep field, use its local pale outline. Disabled buttons have opacity (0.6) and a not-allowed cursor. No special pressed animation is established.

### Navigation
A small personal identity with the offer doing the talking.
- Sticky, opaque page-colored header with a one-pixel bottom rule. The monogram is an ink tile with a small accent dot; it is not an availability-status claim.
- Muted links become accent-colored and underlined on hover or `aria-current="location"`. A separate email link and round theme control sit at the right on desktop.
- On mobile, the email shortcut hides. Opening the menu exposes the links within the header, updates the button's label/expanded state, and moves focus to the first link. Forward Tab traverses the links, theme toggle, and menu trigger, then exits naturally into the page and closes the menu. Escape closes it and returns focus to the trigger; selection, outside click, or breakpoint change also closes it. Without JavaScript, links remain exposed; this is not a modal drawer or focus trap.

### Inline tags
Plain evidence context, not filter controls.
- Unboxed, wrapping technology lists in muted ink, with horizontal/vertical gaps (16px / 6px), line-height (1.65), and the metadata size. No fill, border, pill silhouette, selected state, or hover lift. The hero's smaller stack instead separates items with slashes; the profile retains grouped lists.

### Third-party marks
Identity evidence, not endorsement or a second site palette.
- The employment strip uses locally hosted official Domain and carsales SVGs, with source geometry and colors preserved. Domain uses the supplied white variant in the dark theme. No grayscale, recoloring, filters, cropping of artwork, or fabricated partner badges. See `public/assets/brands/SOURCES.md` for provenance, empty-artboard optimization, and usage limitations.
- The strip explicitly says employment experience, not endorsement. Its four names use a compact single row on desktop and two columns on narrow mobile. MONEYME and Nowcom retain text treatments in the existing font family where a reusable mark was not established.
- The toolkit heading carries the official .NET primary square at (56px), with (16px) clear space. It stays out of the small hero stack and technology tags, preserving the official (50px) minimum. The square's purple and white are third-party artwork colors, not new UI tokens. No AWS mark is used: factual AWS references remain plain text under its published guidance.
- Each image carries its real name as alternative text and explicit intrinsic dimensions. CSS handles the supplied Domain variants; optional content entries fall back to text without JavaScript. Printed employer marks become names; the redundant toolkit mark is omitted from print. Keep the offer, skills, career, and case-study content readable as ordinary text.

### Offer summaries, work evidence, and process
Compact editorial patterns, not a repeated card wall.
- Offers use a top hairline, small teal SVG beside a title, one short description, and a quieter section link. Top padding is (28px), narrowing to (24px); there is no separate deliverable paragraph in this rendered pattern.
